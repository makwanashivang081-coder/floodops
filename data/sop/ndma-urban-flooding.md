# NDMA-aligned urban flooding response notes

Excerpted principles for planner alignment (public guideline themes — not a full legal reprint).
Source themes: NDMA urban flooding guidelines; municipal monsoon preparedness practice.

## Before peak rain
- Pre-position pumps at known chronic waterlogging spots.
- Desilt storm drains and nalla mouths in low-lying wards.
- Keep tide + rainfall watch for coastal cities.

## During waterlogging
- Prioritise life safety and access for emergency vehicles.
- Barricade submerged underpasses and deep carriageways.
- Deploy pumps where depth blocks arterial movement.
- Treat unverified complaints as lower priority until evidence exists.

## After the spell
- Record outcomes: resolved, escalated, false alarm.
- Feed resolved chronic spots back into next season’s blackspot memory.
- Do not claim flood prevention — claim faster, fairer response prioritisation.

## Allowed municipal actions in this product
`pump` · `desilt` · `barricade` · `monitor`
